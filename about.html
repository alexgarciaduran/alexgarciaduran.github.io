About

Research interests

I want to understand what computations neural systems perform, and what makes those computations robust or fragile. I approach this with tools from machine learning and probabilistic inference.

My work spans probabilistic graphical models, recurrent and convolutional neural networks, and biologically constrained vision models, with a recent focus on adversarial robustness: what breaks these networks, and what that reveals about the computations they actually perform.

I am a PhD student at the Centre de Recerca Matemàtica (CRM) and Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC) in Barcelona, supervised by Alexandre Hyafil (CRM) and Manuel Molano-Mazón (UPC).
